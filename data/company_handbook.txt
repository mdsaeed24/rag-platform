Acme Company Employee Handbook

Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Employees must submit leave requests at least 7 days before the requested leave date.
Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
Employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM during remote work days.
Remote work arrangements require approval from the employee's manager.

Parental Leave Policy

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Employees should notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start of parental leave.

Password Security Policy

Employees must change their company password every 90 days.
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
Employees must not share passwords with other employees.

Expense Policy

Business expenses above $500 require manager approval.
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase.
Receipts are required for expenses above $25.
